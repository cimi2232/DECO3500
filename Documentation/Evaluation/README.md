# Evaluation

Material from our two rounds of user testing, taken from our team Miro board.



## Testing round 1 (Week 8, studio)

Peers tried the Find One Friend flow in the Figma prototype with coloured plastic wristbands.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| [week8-protocol-page1.png](week8-protocol-page1.png) to [page4](week8-protocol-page4.png) | The evaluation protocol: study overview, research objectives and questions, participants, testing roles, session procedure, post-task interview, TAM questionnaire, analysis plan and decision signals |
| [week8-key-findings.png](week8-key-findings.png) | The key findings and the new idea that followed: give people a choice of artist-themed icebreakers instead of asking everyone to dance |

## Testing round 2 (Week 11, outside the Zara Larsson concert at Riverstage)
